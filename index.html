About

Daily Fuel

Food values from USDA FoodData Central, chains' published nutrition and Open Food Facts. Everything you log stays on this device. Privacy policy · Source on GitHub

Add food

More nutrients (optional) — fibre, sugar, sodium, saturated fat

Food list: USDA FoodData Central (12,000+ foods). Fast food: each chain's official published nutrition (Australia, UK & Europe, US). Recipes: 2,000 recipes with nutrition calculated from their USDA ingredients (type "recipe" to see only recipes). Packaged products: Open Food Facts.

Add to meal plan

Tap a recipe to add one serve to the chosen day and meal. Add it again for a second serve.

New recipe

Nutrition per serve is calculated from the ingredients' USDA values, the same way as the built-in recipes. Weigh ingredients raw where you can.

Import backup

Merge keeps everything you have here and adds the backup's days, foods and meals. Replace swaps all current data for the backup. Either way the current data is kept so you can undo from Settings.
