Daily Fuel — privacy policy

Daily Fuel is a personal calorie, food, water, steps, exercise and weight tracker. It has no accounts and no server of its own.

Where your data lives

Everything you log is stored only on your device: in the browser's storage for the web version, and in the app's private storage for the iOS and Android apps. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Backups are files you create and keep yourself.

Health data (iOS Health / Android Health Connect)

If you allow it, the app reads your step count, weight and active energy from Apple Health or Health Connect to fill in the Steps and Weight cards, and can write a weight you log back to the same store. These values are used only to show your own totals inside the app. They stay on the device and are not transmitted anywhere. You can revoke access at any time in the Health or Health Connect settings, and the app keeps working with manual entry.

Network requests

The app only contacts other services when you use these features: barcode scanning and "Search online" send the barcode or search words to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org); recipe pictures load from the app's own GitHub Pages site. No personal data is included in these requests.

Analytics and advertising

None. There are no trackers, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Erase all data" removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Contact

This is a personal project. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub repository.
